# Data Processing Agreement — required schedules and approval outline

**NOT EXECUTED. NOT A CONTRACT.** No hosted processing service or signed customer DPA has been established by this repository. Use this as counsel's and procurement's checklist, not as a substitute for legal drafting.

## Parties and roles

Identify legal controller and processor entities and their contact/service addresses. Decide which uses (account administration, customer task data, support, security logs) are controller-controlled versus processor-on-instructions. Document instruction hierarchy and exceptions.

## Mandatory schedules

1. **Processing description:** subject matter/duration, purposes, data subjects, categories of personal data including prohibited or special-category cases, authorised operations and instructions.
2. **Security measures:** identity proofing, per-agent scopes, storage encryption, key rotation, backups, access reviews, vulnerability management, logging, intrusion detection, incident response, restore testing, physical controls and employee access restrictions.
3. **Subprocessors:** identity and hosting providers, processing location, advance-notice mechanism, contractual flow-down, objection process and updated register.
4. **International transfers:** territories, transfer mechanism, applicable UK IDTA/Addendum or adequacy decision, transfer risk assessment and onward-transfer controls where needed.
5. **Assistance:** data-subject requests, privacy impact assessments, regulator requests and incident notifications with operational response procedures.
6. **Exit:** data export format, return/deletion schedule, backup deletion limits, legal holds and confirmation evidence.
7. **Audit rights:** independent assurance, customer review process, scope of security evidence and remediations.

## Known implementation gaps

No hosted tenancy/RBAC, per-customer isolation, automatic data erasure, contractual subprocessor register, external audit immutability, verified data residency or demonstrated incident notification SLA. Do not execute a DPA that promises controls not implemented and independently tested. Counsel must approve final Article 28 terms and all schedules before the first hosted processing agreement.
